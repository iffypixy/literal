Terms

Literal is a free notes app. Using it means agreeing to these few terms.

Limits

A note holds up to 1 MB of text, and an account up to 10,000 notes. Don't use Literal to store anything illegal, or try to overload or break it.

Your notes

You own what you write. Literal stores it only to show it to you and sync it between your devices.

No guarantees

Literal is provided as is. It's built never to lose a word, but keep an export of anything you can't afford to lose. If Literal ever changes in a way that matters, or shuts down, it will say so in the app in time for you to export your notes.
