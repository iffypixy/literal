Privacy

Your notes are yours. Blank keeps only what it needs to sync them, and nothing else.

Without an account

Your notes never leave your device. They're stored in this browser and nowhere else.

With an account

  • What's stored: your email address, your notes, and when they were created and edited.
  • Where: on Cloudflare, which runs Blank. Sign-in codes are sent by email through Resend.
  • Who can read your notes: only you. They're encrypted on the way and on disk, but not end to end: whoever runs Blank could technically reach the database. We don't, and never will except to fix something you asked us to.

What Blank doesn't do

  • No ads, no tracking, and no selling or sharing your data.
  • No analytics cookies. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.
  • The only cookie keeps you signed in.

Your control

  • Export every note as .txt files, anytime, from the notes panel.
  • Deleting your account deletes your notes and your email from Blank at once. Copies may remain in Cloudflare's automatic backups for up to 30 days.
