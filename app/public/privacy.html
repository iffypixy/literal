Privacy

Your notes are yours. Literal keeps only what it needs to sync them.

Without an account

Your notes never leave this browser. They're stored on this device and nowhere else.

With an account

  • What's stored: your email address, your notes, and when they were created, edited and deleted.
  • Where: on Cloudflare, which runs Literal. Sign-in codes are sent by email through Resend.
  • Who can read your notes: you. They're encrypted in transit and on disk, but not end to end, so whoever runs Literal could reach them. We don't, unless you ask us to fix something.
  • Deleted notes: kept on the server for 30 days, so your other devices learn they're gone, then erased.
  • Signing in with Google or Apple: Literal keeps only your email address, not your name, your picture, or access to your account there.
  • Your IP address: used for a few minutes to limit sign-in attempts, and not kept with your account.

What Literal doesn't do

  • No ads, no tracking, and no selling or sharing your data.
  • No analytics cookies. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.
  • The only cookies are for signing in.

Your control

  • Export all your notes as .txt files anytime, from the notes list.
  • Deleting your account erases your notes and your email from Literal at once. Copies may remain in Cloudflare's automatic backups for up to 30 days.
