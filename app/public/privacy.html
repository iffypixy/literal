Privacy

Your notes are yours. Blank keeps only what it needs to sync them.

Without an account

Your notes never leave this browser. They're stored on this device and nowhere else.

With an account

  • What's stored: your email address, your notes, and when they were created and edited.
  • Where: on Cloudflare, which runs Blank. Sign-in codes are sent by email through Resend.
  • Who can read your notes: you. They're encrypted in transit and on disk, but not end to end, so whoever runs Blank could reach them. We don't, unless you ask us to fix something.

What Blank doesn't do

  • No ads, no tracking, and no selling or sharing your data.
  • No analytics cookies. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies.
  • The only cookie keeps you signed in.

Your control

  • Export all your notes as .txt files anytime, from the notes list.
  • Deleting your account erases your notes and your email from Blank at once. Copies may remain in Cloudflare's automatic backups for up to 30 days.
